ABOUT ME

A little about
my journey.

01
How It Started

Coding wasn't something I was originally interested in. When I started studying Software Engineering, I honestly wasn't sure how I'd feel about it.

But the more I learned, the more curious I became about how websites and applications actually worked.

02
Learning For Real

At one point, I started relying heavily on AI while coding. It made things easier, but I eventually realized I wasn't understanding as much as I should.

So I decided to slow down and actually learn. I started studying the fundamentals, practicing, building small projects, making mistakes, and figuring things out myself.

It hasn't always been easy, but I can genuinely see the difference between where I started and where I am now.

03
Where I Am Now

I'm still learning, but I'm no longer just trying to make code work. I want to understand it.

One of the things I'm most proud of is this portfolio. I built it from scratch, learning along the way and solving problems myself.

It's not the final version of what I want to create, but it represents how far I've come.
